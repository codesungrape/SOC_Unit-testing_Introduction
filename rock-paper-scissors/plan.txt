
'calculateRoundResult' function takes in the playerMove and computerMove as its arguments

function calculateRoundResult( playerMove, computerMove) {}

options for both playerMove and computerMove will always be 'rock' or 'paper' or 'scissors'

depending on these two args (playerMove, computerMove), the fucntion calculateRoundResult() returns an object containing:
1. OUTCOME 1 (win/loss)
    - outcome: who is winner?
    - message: `Player chose [rock/paper/scissors] and computer chose [rock/paper/scissors]. [Player wins/Computer wins].`
2. OUTCOME 2 (draw)
    - outcome: who is winner?
    - message: `Both players chose [rock/paper/scissors]. It's a draw.`

The normal rules of rock paper scissors apply i.e. rock beats scissors, scissors beat paper, paper beats rock.

If either of the human move or computer move is unsupported/invalid, then an error should be thrown.

FUNCTION generateComputerMove() returns a random move
- either [ROCK, PAPER, scissors]
